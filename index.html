A Personal Chapter

Moon 🌙

Vishesh ki story mein Moon ek important emotional chapter raha. Padhai par focus karne ke liye ek waqt par baat-cheet se distance liya gaya, aur baad mein kuch cheezein waise nahi nikli jaisi sochi gayi thi. Is chapter se ek lesson mila: feelings ko respect karna, doosre person ki choice ka respect karna, aur apni life ko rukne na dena.

Ye chapter growth ka bhi part hai — feelings apni jagah, respect apni jagah, aur future apni jagah.
